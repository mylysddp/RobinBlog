## Signs your Threads profile needs a reset
- Too many off-topic posts from past projects.
- Replies or reposts that no longer match your tone.
- A timeline that feels inconsistent or confusing.

## The curated cleanup flow
1. Head to https://deletethreads.net/ and select the content type to review.
2. Filter by time period or keywords that no longer fit.
3. Preview the list and remove the clutter.

## Keep the timeline curated with automation
Use DeleteThreads scheduled tasks to run recurring cleanups with the same filters you already trust. This set-it-and-forget-it approach removes the need for daily manual deletion and is a standout capability compared to most cleanup tools on the market.

## Keep it curated over time
- Do a quarterly sweep to stay consistent.
- Remove launch-related posts once the campaign ends.
- Keep a short list of evergreen posts you never delete.
